● Security & Trust

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At Questy, privacy and research integrity are foundational. Questy operates with local-first and zero-knowledge telemetry principles:

  • Zero Sensitive Data Ingestion: Questy does not log, transmit, or monetize your source code, private git repositories, or proprietary prompts.
  • Local Execution: All R&D scoring rubrics and analytical rules evaluate entirely within your designated client workspace.
  • Authentication & Credentials: API keys, Git credentials, and personal tokens are never stored in plain text or centralized servers.

For questions or security disclosures, contact security@questy.ai.
